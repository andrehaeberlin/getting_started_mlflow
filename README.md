# getting_started_mlflow

For those new to MLflow or seeking a refresher on its core functionalities, the quickstart tutorials here are the perfect starting point. They will guide you step-by-step through fundamental concepts, focusing purely on a task that will maximize your understanding of how to use MLflow to solve a particular task.

This material is a modified copy of [https://mlflow.org/docs/latest/getting-started/logging-first-model/step2-mlflow-client.html]

## Tutorial sequence

The numbered Jupyter notebooks cover the tracking server, MLflow Client API, creation and search of experiments, preparation of an apple dataset, logging runs, and client initialization. Additional notebooks introduce tracking, logging a first model, and basic authentication.

Open the notebooks in Jupyter and follow their individual setup instructions. A tracking server and compatible MLflow environment are required for the tracking exercises.

This is learning material rather than a production MLflow deployment. Keep environment secrets and local authentication databases out of shared copies of the project.
